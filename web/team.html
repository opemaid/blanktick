Founder

Simphiwe Damoyi — Founder & CEO

Simphiwe founded Blanktick and is responsible for everything it ships: product, engineering, security and operations. He has worked in enterprise IT since 2015, and since 2017 as a Microsoft 365 messaging, collaboration and security specialist in a large organisation. That work covers email security, identity, Zero Trust network access and governance.

He holds a BBA (Honours) from Maharishi University of Management and Microsoft certifications including Azure Administrator, Azure Security Technologies and Security Operations Analyst. He is based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

Team, staff & advisors

Blanktick is a single-founder company. Simphiwe is currently its only director and team member. There are no other employees, advisors, board members or outside investors. When that changes, they will be listed on this page.

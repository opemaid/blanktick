What Blanktick is, and is not

  • It is not an antivirus and does not replace one. It has no file scanner, no signatures and no real-time protection. Keep Microsoft Defender, or your existing antivirus, switched on.
  • A clean result is not a guarantee. It means the checks that ran found nothing. The checks that could not run are listed by name on the same screen.
  • It reports and never changes anything. Acting on a finding is your decision. If a device belongs to your employer, check with your IT team before changing its settings.

Use it on your own devices

Run Blanktick on computers and phones you own, or that you are authorised to examine. It reads security settings, and reading them on someone else’s device without permission may be unlawful where you live.

The download

The Windows build is not code-signed, so Windows will warn you before it runs. Check the file’s SHA-256 hash against the one published on the download section, or build it from source, before you trust it.

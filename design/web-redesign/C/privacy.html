The short version: the Blanktick app collects nothing and sends nothing. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from anyone else. The rest of this page explains the few places where information about you does exist, and who holds it.

Who we are

Blanktick is a free, open-source project founded and run by Simphiwe Damoyi in South Africa. For anything on this page, write to hello@blanktick.org.

The app

  • No data leaves your device. The Android build has no internet permission, so the operating system refuses it a network connection. The desktop build is checked at build time to contain no networking code. There is no account, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no update check.
  • Scan results stay where they are produced. Blanktick writes one settings file next to itself. It keeps no history on any server, because there is no server.
  • The password and link tools are local. What you type is compared against lists bundled inside the download. It is not stored and not transmitted.
  • One exception, which we would rather tell you about. On Windows, Blanktick asks Microsoft Defender to scan roughly seventy files. Depending on the cloud-protection setting you chose in Windows Security, Defender may contact Microsoft about them. That is Defender’s connection, under Microsoft’s privacy terms and your settings, not ours, but it happens because Blanktick asked.

This website

  • No cookies. blanktick.org sets none, first-party or third-party, so there is no cookie banner to dismiss.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no embeds, no third-party webfonts. The site’s security policy forbids every script and every third-party request. If our hosting provider ever tries to insert one, your browser blocks it. You can see this for yourself in your browser’s network tab.
  • Hosting. The site and the download (dl.blanktick.org) are served by Cloudflare. To deliver a page, Cloudflare necessarily processes your IP address, the address requested and your browser’s user-agent, and it keeps short-lived request logs that we can use to fix problems. We do not use them to identify or profile visitors. Cloudflare’s handling of that data is described in its own privacy policy.

If you email us

We keep your message and address so we can reply, and for as long as the conversation is useful. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not share what you send with anyone, unless you report a security vulnerability and ask us to credit you.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • Show advertising, or build profiles for anyone who does.
  • Collect anything about your device through the app.

Your rights

Under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and the GDPR if you are in Europe, you can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. In practice the only thing we could hold is an email you sent us. Write to hello@blanktick.org. If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator, or to the data-protection authority where you live.

Children

Blanktick is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app never connects to us, a policy change cannot alter what an already-downloaded copy does.

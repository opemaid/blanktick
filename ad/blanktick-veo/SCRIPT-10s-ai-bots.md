# Blanktick — "AI bots are attacking your PC" (10 s)

Same shots, pacing and blast ending as the monsters ad (`Downloads\Untitled video (3).mp4`).
The cute monsters become **small menacing AI bots**; Blanktick is the cream tile with the kelp broken
tick (`logo-1024.png`), and its energy is cream-white with kelp-green edges. No blue, no shield.

Why it's a true ad: Blanktick's Live protection flags an AI assistant being driven to act on your PC,
and the check-up finds AI tools given permission to run programs (homepage: "Risky AI tools",
"AI tool safety"). Say **catches**, not "blocks all" or "removes".

## Veo / Flow prompt — clip A (8 s)

Upload `logo-1024.png` as an Ingredient. (Don't use the old start frame — it has monsters in it.)

> Dark, cinematic gaming-desk room at night. A glowing gaming PC, monitor and keyboard are swarmed by dozens of small, sleek AI robots — glossy chrome and graphite bodies, single glowing red eyes, tiny blinking antenna lights — crawling over the monitor, keyboard and PC tower, plugging cables into ports. The monitor floods with streaming red code and red warning triangles. At 1 second one robot leaps toward the camera, its red eye scanning the lens. High-detail 3D, slightly playful, Pixar-meets-sci-fi.
>
> At 2 seconds, Blanktick's logo appears on the right of frame: a thick, matte cream-coloured square tile (#FBFAF6) with a bold deep kelp-green (#0F4D3F) checkmark whose long arm is broken by a small clean gap, exactly as in the reference image. Flat, sharp corners, not a shield, no blue. It glows with warm cream light and the robots turn to look at it.
>
> At 4 seconds, close-up on the tile charging with crackling cream-white energy with kelp-green edges. At 5 seconds it fires sweeping beams of that energy across the desk; at 6 seconds the blast hits the PC; at 7 seconds the robots are knocked off, glitching and breaking apart into sparkling pixels, their red eyes switching off, the red code vanishing. In the final second the monitor turns warm cream and shows neat rows of small kelp-green checkmarks.
>
> Camera: slow push-in, close-up on the tile, whip-pan along the blast. Lighting: cold dark room with red robot glow; Blanktick's cream-and-green light is the only warm light. No readable text, no letters anywhere.
>
> Audio: robotic chirps and glitchy digital chatter, a rising alarm, a bright "ting" as the tile appears, a charging hum, a big whoosh-boom for the blast, glitch-pops as robots vanish, a calm resolved chord.
>
> Negative: shield, blue light, blue lightning, neon blue, padlock, humanoid robots, gore, text, letters, words, logo lettering.

Generate 3–4 takes; keep the one where nothing is blue and the tile is cream with a green tick.

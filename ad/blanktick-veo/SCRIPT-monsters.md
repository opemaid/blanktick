# Blanktick — "Monsters" remake (10 s, 16:9, Google Veo / Flow)

This is the same ad as `Downloads\Untitled video (3).mp4` (the monster-swarmed PC). Only Blanktick changes: the blue shield becomes the new brand.

## The only things that change

| Old | New |
|---|---|
| Blue glowing shield with "Blanktick" on it | A thick **cream tile (#FBFAF6)** carrying the **broken tick in deep kelp green (#0F4D3F)**. Its long arm has a small gap in it. This is `logo-1024.png`, the same mark as the website and app. |
| Electric-blue lightning beam | A **warm cream light sweep** with **kelp-green** edges. No blue anywhere on Blanktick. |
| Green "Protected" screen with "All threats neutralized" | The PC screen turns **cream** with rows of kelp ticks and blank ticks, like the website's scan receipt. |
| Blue shield end card | `endcard-1920x1080.png`: the logo plus **Download free at blanktick.org**. |
| Garbled captions ("Blanking your fesakt!", "Honting four lerne!") | Removed. Captions are added in the edit, so they are spelled right. |

The desk, PC, monsters, camera moves and pacing all stay the same. The room can stay dark, but Blanktick is the one warm cream-and-green thing in it, so it pops harder than the blue did.

**One honest tweak:** in the original the monsters are blasted away and the screen says "all threats neutralized". Your website says Blanktick is not an antivirus and removes nothing, so here the sweep **finds** the monsters: they freeze, and each gets a little kelp tag. The beat still lands. If you want the original blast-them-away version anyway, say so and I'll write that prompt; it's your call.

## Brand block — paste into every prompt

> Blanktick's logo is a thick, matte cream-coloured square tile (#FBFAF6) with a bold deep kelp-green (#0F4D3F) checkmark on it whose long arm is broken by a small clean gap — exactly as in the reference image. Flat, sharp-cornered, butt-ended strokes, no bevel, no shield shape, no blue, no electric glow. Its light is warm cream with kelp-green edges. Do not write any letters or words anywhere.

**Reference image:** upload `logo-1024.png` as an Ingredient in Flow, so Veo copies the exact tick.

## Clip 1 (8 s): Veo prompt

> Dark, cosy gaming-desk room at night. A glowing gaming PC and monitor are swarmed by dozens of cute, fluffy, colourful cartoon monsters — orange, pink, purple, teal, lime — climbing over the monitor, the keyboard and the PC tower, giggling and causing mischief, with red warning triangles flashing on the monitor. Pixar-style 3D, playful, high detail.
>
> At 2 seconds, Blanktick's logo appears on the right of frame: [BRAND BLOCK]. It rises into view as a solid, glossy-matte cream tile, catching warm light.
>
> At 4 seconds, the tile sends out a wide, sweeping beam of warm cream light with kelp-green edges across the desk, like a scanner pass. Every monster the light touches freezes mid-pose, surprised, and a small kelp-green tag pops up above its head. The red warning triangles on the monitor turn off one by one.
>
> At 7 seconds, the monitor screen turns warm cream and fills with neat rows of small kelp-green checkmarks and empty outlined checkmarks. The frozen monsters sheepishly hop off the desk.
>
> Camera: slow push-in at the start, then a whip-pan along the beam. Lighting: cool dark room, Blanktick is the only warm cream-and-green light source. No readable text anywhere.
>
> Audio: playful monster giggles and chaos, a bright "ting" when the tile appears, a soft whoosh for the sweep, little "boop" sounds as each monster freezes, a calm resolved chord at the end.

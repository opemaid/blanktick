## Claims check

- "Free" and "blanktick.org" — on the homepage.
- "Catches ransomware and hackers" — Live protection detects ransomware, password theft and hacker remote control (homepage feature card). Say *catches*, not *blocks all* or *removes*.
- "Shows what it couldn't check" — the product's core promise.
- Do not say: "replaces your antivirus", "100% protection", "removes viruses".
